The intraday agent with Draft 2's experiments inside it

What the Draft 3 intraday agent would look like if it also ran Draft 2's small, reversible experiments during the day. Two diagrams, the mechanism in plain words, what it would add to the story, what it would cost, and when it would be worth doing.

The mechanism, in plain words

Holding a plan precisely is only worth something if the plan is right, and on day one nobody knows. So when the room is calm enough to change and busy enough to measure, the agent tries one small nudge on one lever, a step a guest would not notice, ramped in as usual, and watches the one thing the room cannot hide: how fast people leave.

The numbers Draft 2 used
  • Step size: 60 lux, 0.5 °C or 3 dB. One lever at a time.
  • Settle 10 min for the ramp to land, then a 30 min trial window.
  • Measure: departures per occupant-minute in the trial versus the 30 min before.
  • Evidence bar: at least 5 people in the room and 120 occupant-minutes in each window, about 12 departures.
  • Result: a 20% change in leave rate counts. Smaller is unclear.
  • Discipline: one experiment per block, none within 45 min of a block change, never during an owner override.
The three verdicts
keepLeave rate clearly lower. The nudge stays for the rest of the block and carries into the next one. That direction is worth another step later. revertLeave rate clearly higher. Undo it. That direction is marked bad for today. unclearToo few people or no clear difference. Undo it, remember nothing.

Verdicts accumulate as a score per direction, plus one for each keep, minus one for each revert, from today and from past days. The next nudge picks the best-scoring untried direction. A kept nudge is re-tested the next time that direction comes up, which is the protection against a lucky half hour.

What it would add to the Draft 3 story

Learning inside the day

Today the navigation team learns nothing until the analysis team runs after close. With experiments, by dinner the agent already knows one thing about this room from this room, with a number behind it. That is the "adapt" verb happening on camera, without waiting a night.

Evidence for the analysis team

Every experiment is a small before-and-after on one lever with two leave rates and a verdict. Written into the master record with set_by = experiment, they are exactly the empirical evidence Lionel's chains need. A kept nudge could even enter the question bank as a ready-made hypothesis: "dimmer light keeps people longer here", with three trials attached.

A better baseline tomorrow

Kept nudges seed the next morning's offsets, so the baseline tool stops being a median of habit and starts being a median of what worked. Right now the baseline can only reproduce yesterday.

Fits the sketch

Nothing in Lionel's whiteboard forbids it. The navigation team still owns "what should the room be right now"; it just gets a second, faster source of answers next to memory. The memory agent and question bank stay the only bridge to the analysis team.

What it would cost, and what could go wrong

ItemDetail
Model callsNone. Explore and judge are plain code, the same as Draft 2. Cost per day stays at about 3 cents.
CodePort explore.py (180 lines) and the leave-rate judge from Draft 2 into intraday_agent.py as two graph nodes, one push reason, one master-record value. About 2 to 3 hours with tests.
NoiseA 30-minute window holds about 10 departures, so single verdicts are noisy. Draft 2's protection: re-test kept nudges, and require the evidence bar. Still the weakest part; say so if it goes in the deck.
InterferenceAn experiment and a discrepancy rewrite must never overlap: a discrepancy aborts the trial and marks it unclear. Otherwise the leave rate would be judging the rain, not the nudge.
Owner overridesNever experiment inside an override window. Already easy: the block carries the owner's reason.
The simulationDead on arrival until the room fills. With 2 of 30 seats the evidence bar is never met and no experiment ever starts. This is the same blocker as the eval numbers.
Token trackingUnchanged. Experiments add rows to the master record and pushes, not to the tokens file.

When it would be worth doing

Not before the demo

It adds a third kind of push to record and explain, and its verdicts are only visible on a room that fills. Sunday's time is better spent on the simulation preset and one real verdict from the analysis team.

After the demo, if the simulation is fixed

It is the cheapest path to visible in-day learning, it costs no tokens, and it hands the analysis team pre-run experiments instead of open questions. If the team wants the agent to "get better at it over days" to be true of the navigation team too, this is how.
